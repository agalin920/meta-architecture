# Counting inventions (and copies) as the evidence

**Status:** `retired` (ruling 1; `REDESIGN.md:50-53`).

**Lesson.** The old bar made a pattern `proven` only when two systems invented
it independently, and spent its effort sorting copies, carries and inherited
presence out of that count. Systems born from the playbook can never re-invent what
they inherited, so the bar could barely turn. What counts now is a trial: a
lesson tried in a second system and judged by that system's scoreboard.

**Evidence**
- **Nine of ten** old `proven` patterns failed the bar at review 001:
  `archive/meta/architect-log.md:3243`.
- The more systems are born, the fewer independent tests of the spine exist:
  `archive/meta/architect-log.md:3248-3256`.
- One promotion in 80 days under the bar (`honest-metric`, 2026-08-18):
  `archive/meta/architect-log.md:2060-2064`.
- Convergence that was not: both mPulse teams dropped the same marker on the
  same afternoon, from one parent session, each citing the other:
  `archive/meta/architect-log.md:3384-3391`.

**What stays true**
- **Presence is not evidence.** A copy, an inherited invariant or a mechanism
  present but never fired proves nothing:
  `archive/meta/architect-log.md:410-414`; `archive/patterns/README.md:120-123`.
- **Copies lose the invisible part:** both mPulse teams lost the `unmeasured`
  category they inherited: `archive/patterns/README.md:142-145`.
- A carried trial counts as a trial, not as an invention (`../playbook.md`).

**Where it failed / limits**
- It did stop this layer promoting its own proposals by carrying them, which
  was a real hole: `archive/patterns/README.md:206-210`. The new rule closes it
  differently: the trial is judged by the receiving system's scoreboard.

**Origin.** This layer, 2026-08-09 to 2026-08-18.
