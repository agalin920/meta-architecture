# Predict-then-check: the human half (student prediction)

**Status:** `retired` (cut 2026-08-09). The system half lives on in the loop.

**Lesson.** Asking the human to predict his own score each session failed on
first use and was cut. Split any pattern by who pays: the half that costs the
system nothing survives; the half that costs the human a step every cycle
does not.

**Evidence**
- First use failed: "I have no clue what you are asking me"; a reworded
  replacement never ran, and the student cut the mechanism:
  `personal-university: meta/calibration.md:46-75`.
- Even before the cut, 10 of 15 sessions sealed no prediction; a bias of 0.00
  on n=5 is "arithmetic, not knowledge":
  `personal-university: meta/architect-log.md:1061-1075`.
- The signal was already collected more cheaply: the one-word friction tag
  answered the same question by observation:
  `archive/patterns/predict-then-check.md:44-47`.

**Where it failed / limits**
- **Cost recorded honestly:** capacity 3 of that thesis lost its only direct
  training mechanism: `archive/patterns/predict-then-check.md:51-52`.
- **The system half was never ruled out** and is now in the kernel: every
  self-change states its expected effect and is checked against it. See
  [grade-previous-cycle](grade-previous-cycle.md).
- Reintroduction bar: a mechanism that costs the human nothing at the point of
  use; a rewording does not qualify:
  `archive/patterns/predict-then-check.md:66-68`.

**Origin.** personal-university, 2026-08. One of the three cuts in
