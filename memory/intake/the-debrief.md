`~/dev/the-debrief`, founded 2026-08-24 (`1e143f2`). Builds **The Debrief** — an
interactive corpus of every discovery, invention, work and mind that changed what
humanity knew, chronological and geographically anchored. **The corpus is the
product; the site is how it is read.** No git remote; `gh repo create` denied at
its own permission layer, so creating one is Andres's call — same as
[[galind-team]].

**The third birth, and the first one this layer ran itself.** The two prior births
had not read each other; this session had read the spine, the catalog and both
birth entries in full. **Every choice it shares with a sibling is a `carried`
adoption and is evidence for nothing.** Three are labelled as carries in the
child's own `docs/decision-log.md` — most importantly `D-006`, whose ledger is a
**third** instance of the improvement-versus-delivery ratio family. **Do not count
it as a third adopter**; that promotion-gate candidate still stands at two.

**What the birth was actually worth: the first system in the portfolio whose human
gate cannot scale with its output.** Thousands of factual claims, one reviewer.
`patterns/human-gate.md` is about *irreversibility* and is silent on *unreviewable
volume* — a different failure, because the git history is perfectly revertible and
the gate fails anyway. Its answer is a bet (`D-002`): the gate is on the **slice**,
and the independent adversary **nominates a worst-case gate sample** of 8–12
entries it could not confirm. Graded by one number — did the Director's findings
come from inside the sample or outside it. **A spine question for review 001, not
a dispatchable finding**: no existing child has the condition.

**Found at founding and deliberately not settled:** `personal-university` covers
overlapping subject matter and holds the opposite pedagogy, in writing —
*"Organized by mechanism, not by chronology... No survey courses"* and *"A
connection handed over is a nice sentence he'll forget."* The Debrief is a
chronological survey whose headline feature hands the reader the connections.
**Nothing was dispatched to PU and it was not corrected** — the layer never owns a
system's domain. The tension lives in the new system's own thesis, quoted, and its
Architect must treat it disappearing as a finding.

Other founding facts worth not rediscovering: zero corpus entries by design (a
session in the meta layer writing entries would be doing a child's domain work);
`tools/audit.py` is dependency-free Python with a `selftest` because there is no
corpus to validate; the ledger falsified its own first reading on day one, the
third instrument in the portfolio to do so.
