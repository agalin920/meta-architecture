# Falsifiable thesis

**Status:** `candidate`. It changed decisions on record in two systems; no
number moved because of it.

**Lesson.** Keep one page, apart from the charter, saying why the system is
shaped as it is and which results would prove that shape wrong. With
falsifiers a mechanism can be killed on evidence; without them it can only be
tuned, so additions become permanent.

**Evidence**
- personal-university: when the student prediction was cut, the thesis made
  the cost visible: capacity 3 marked "not currently trained", §III half true,
  the discharged falsifier replaced:
  `archive/patterns/falsifiable-thesis.md:34-38`.
- meta-architecture: claim IV's falsifier was met seven reviews running and
  review 001 acted on it (M-2); claim III "bit with data" for the first time:
  `archive/meta/architect-log.md:3374-3376`, `:3435-3438`. That evidence is
  part of why this redesign exists (`REDESIGN.md:40-61`), and the redesign
  keeps a falsifier for itself (`REDESIGN.md:296-299`).

**Where it failed / limits**
- **Slow to act.** Claim IV was falsified seven times before anything changed.
- **Falsifiers chosen after the shot** are decoration: a birth count was
  treated as success after births existed; the sharper falsifier was drafted
  late and applies only forward: `archive/meta/architect-log.md:2297-2311`,
  `:3425-3432`.
- Only personal-university and this layer ever had one; none of the
  engineering teams did: `archive/patterns/falsifiable-thesis.md:39-42`.

**Origin.** personal-university, 2026-08; this layer's `meta/thesis.md`.
